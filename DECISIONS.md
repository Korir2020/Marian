# School AI Platform — Decisions

## 1. Development Approach
Build the platform in small, tested stages rather than attempting everything at once.

## 2. Core Principle
The school management system must work even when AI services are unavailable.

## 3. AI
AI will be separated from the core system through an AI service/gateway.

## 4. Security
Important actions and official school records must require appropriate authorization.

## 5. Data
Student and school data must be protected and access-controlled.

## 6. Future Expansion
The architecture should allow mobile apps, offline operation, payments, voice, local languages, and advanced AI features to be added later.
